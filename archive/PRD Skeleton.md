## Problem Statement

30-day retention has dropped to 37%, down from 44% last quarter. Data shows churn is sharpest among users who don't set a savings goal in week 1 (nearly double the churn rate of those who do). User research confirms: users who connect their account and view the dashboard don't have a clear next step after the initial spending breakdown. Beyond week 1, the home feed doesn't change based on user activity or tenure — it looks the same whether someone opened the app yesterday or three weeks ago, so there's nothing pulling lapsed users back. Separately, the weekly summary email has a decent 22% open rate, but the in-app experience doesn't continue the story the email starts, creating a jarring disconnect.

Working hypothesis: users go passive because the app stops feeling relevant after the first "aha moment." There's no mechanism bringing them back with a reason specific to their own behavior.

## Goals

- Give users a reason to return to the app that's personalized to their own activity, not generic tips.
- Continue the narrative started by the weekly summary email into the in-app experience.
- Surface actionable, personalized insights (e.g., top insight of the week, a nudge based on actual patterns, progress toward savings goal).
