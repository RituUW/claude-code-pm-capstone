# PM Brief — Engage v2 Prototype

## User

28-year-old who connected their Chase account 2 weeks ago and has not opened the app since. Set a savings goal during onboarding.

## Job to Be Done

Understand where their money went this week and take one action.

## Feature

Personalized weekly money summary — top spending insight, one contextual nudge, savings goal progress.

## Constraint

Use data Nudge already has — no new integrations.
